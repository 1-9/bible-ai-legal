This Privacy Policy explains how Bible AI: Study Assistant ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Account and purchase data: if you subscribe, Google Play processes your payment and shares limited purchase/subscription status information (e.g. an anonymized purchase token, subscription tier, renewal status) with us via Google Play Billing and our subscription infrastructure (RevenueCat).
  • App usage data: basic, non-identifying analytics such as feature usage, crash reports, and session activity, used to improve the App.
  • AI query content: questions or text you submit to the in-app AI study assistant are sent to our AI processing provider(s) to generate a response. We do not require you to submit personal information in these queries, and you should avoid including sensitive personal details in your questions.
  • Device information: device type, operating system version, and language/locale settings, used for compatibility and support purposes.

We do not require account registration with an email or password to use core features of the App.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's features, including the AI study assistant;
  • To process and manage subscriptions and free trials;
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve reliability and performance;
  • To understand aggregate usage trends and improve the App's content and features.

3. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Billing — processes subscription payments. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status and entitlements. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • AI processing provider(s) — process the text of your questions to generate study assistant responses. Query content may be transmitted to these providers solely to generate a response and is not used by us to build an advertising profile.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain usage and diagnostic data only as long as necessary to provide the App's functionality and to comply with legal obligations. Subscription records are retained as required by Google Play and applicable tax/accounting law.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete information associated with you. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time via the Google Play Store subscriptions page. To request deletion of any data we hold about you, contact us using the details below.
